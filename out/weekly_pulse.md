# Groww Weekly Product Pulse — 13 Oct 2026

**Input:** 500 Play Store reviews (India, English), 21 Jul – 19 Sep 2026 · 51% rated 1–2★

## Top themes (ranked by 1–2★ volume)
1. **App reliability** — 189 reviews (38%), avg 2.4★. Chart-screen regressions after updates, stale prices, stuck orders.
2. **Customer support** — 99 reviews (20%), avg 1.4★. Long IVR holds, AI-bot loops, tickets closed unresolved.
3. **Charges & deductions** — 71 reviews (14%), avg 2.1★. Deductions exceed the "₹20 brokerage" users expect.

Bright spot: 144 reviews (29%, 4.8★) praise its simplicity for beginners.

## Voice of the user
- "When we open the charts the back button on our phone just disappears and we end up clicking the scalper mode or …" (1★, 28 Aug)
- "they keep you on hold for more than 10 mins and never connect." (1★, 1 Aug)
- "I understand that taxes and other charges may apply, but the deductions were very disappointing and were not clear enough to me." (1★, 19 Sep)

## Key observation
App reliability complaints peaked 24–30 Aug (50% of that week's reviews); 30 reviews mention the Scalper button or hidden back-navigation (20 rated 1–2★). "External charges & taxes" is the most confused charge: 16 of 24 mentions signal confusion, not price objection.

## Action ideas
1. **Chart hotfix:** move the Scalper toggle away from Back, keep the nav bar visible, add an opt-out.
2. **Support SLA:** show real queue times, offer callback, no ticket closure without a human reply.
3. **Charge clarity:** itemized pre-trade charge preview; link the fee explainer from P&L.
